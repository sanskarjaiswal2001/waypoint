# Issue #7: novice usability study

This is a study guide, not a claim that human testing has happened. Recruit five people unfamiliar with Waypoint who do not have technical backgrounds. Use the candidate APK on a stationary phone, with public demonstration places and fictional tasks. Do not coach the participant through controls.

Ask each person to:

1. Find Quests, add “Pick up groceries” and complete it within one minute.
2. Attach a saved public place to another quest and open navigation preview.
3. Start, pause and resume a focus session.
4. Share a place into Waypoint and identify the proposed destination before navigating.
5. Find a new world, preview it, and apply it.
6. Explain how rewards are earned and whether navigation needs to be unlocked.

Repeat the relevant alert step with notification permission denied. The participant should still understand how to continue without alerts. Use large text for at least one run.

Record completion, time, wrong turns and the participant’s own explanation. Do not record personal addresses, task text or identifying information in the repository. The target is at least four of five completing each task without coaching. Revise flows that fail the target before describing them as validated for novice users.

Automated UI tests, developer walkthroughs and a simulated persona do not substitute for these five participants.
