# Your tasks, with rewards

Open **Quests** in the main navigation bar. Tap **Add a quest**, enter a title and tap **Add quest**. Nothing else is required. **Everyday task**, **Visit a place** and **Focus session** are optional starting points.

## Add details when they help

Open a task and choose **Edit**. **Add details** reveals notes, a place, a reminder, a focus timer and an optional character name or picture. Choose an existing Saved place or search for one. **Navigate** opens the usual destination preview; arrival never marks the task complete.

Choose a built-in portrait or **Choose a photo**. Android’s photo picker gives Waypoint only the selected photo. Review the square centre crop before saving. The resized picture is re-encoded without photo metadata and kept in private app storage. It is never uploaded.

Tap **Complete quest** yourself. **Undo** reverses a mistaken completion, including its XP and achievement credit. Completed tasks also offer **Reopen quest**. Deleting a completed task removes its notes and portrait while keeping an anonymous reward record, so tidying the list does not remove earned progress.

## Focus and reminders

Choose 15, 25 or 45 minutes, or a custom duration from 1 to 180 minutes. Only one session can run or be paused at a time. Use **Start focus**, **Pause**, **Resume** or **Finish early**. Finishing early does not earn a focus bonus.

Closing the screen or app keeps the timer. Changing the phone’s clock cannot advance a running session. After a reboot, Waypoint restores the remaining time; an expired session asks **Did you finish your focus time?** before counting focus credit. Finishing a timer still leaves task completion up to you.

Enable **Alert me when time is up** to receive a background alert. Waypoint asks for notification access only when you enable alerts. If access was previously declined, **Open notification settings** provides a way to enable it. Android 12 and later also offer **Enable on-time alerts**. Without that access, the timer still works but an alert may arrive late while the phone sleeps.

Navigation, Quest reminders and Focus alerts have separate Android notification channels. Your sound and Do Not Disturb choices apply. During driving navigation or Android Auto use, nonessential quest notifications are silent and in-app reward celebrations wait. Quest editing is a phone feature, outside active guidance.

## Your journey

Open the profile button at the top of Quests to see rewards and settings. One profile works across every world.

| Progress | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Explicitly complete a quest | 20 XP |
| Finish its focus session of at least ten minutes | 10 extra XP, once per quest |
| Every 100 XP | One level |
| First quest, ten quests, fifty quests, five focus sessions | Achievement badges |
| Levels 2, 5 and 10 | Avatar frames |
| Five, twenty and fifty completed quests | Short story chapters |

All map features and worlds are available immediately. There are no streak penalties, leaderboards or travel-speed rewards. Hide progression or turn off in-app completion sounds without losing tasks or earned progress.

## Move places between apps

- Share address text or a supported Google Maps place link and choose **Waypoint**. Review the proposal before opening it. You can navigate, save it or choose **Add a quest here** from the place’s menu.
- Paste an address or supported link into search. Where the originating app supports Android text actions, select an address and choose **Find in Waypoint**.
- From a place’s menu, choose **Open in another map app**. Google Maps receives the destination and supported travel mode; other geo-compatible apps receive the destination. They calculate their own routes.

An incoming proposal never silently replaces guidance. Unsupported links return you to search with a plain-language explanation. Multi-stop imports, GPX files and exact route transfer are outside this version.

Quests, pictures and progress stay on this phone. Compatible updates preserve them; uninstalling removes them. There is no account, cloud backup or synchronization.
